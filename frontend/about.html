About JobBoard

A simple way to discover opportunities from live job sources.

Discover More Opportunities

JobBoard brings job opportunities from supported external sources into one searchable platform.

Jobs are collected, normalized, and organized so you can explore opportunities by title, location, company, category, and other useful information.

Live Job Data

JobBoard is designed around live job-source integration rather than manually maintained job listings.

Open Source

JobBoard is built as an open-source project so developers can learn from it, improve it, and contribute new features and supported job sources.
